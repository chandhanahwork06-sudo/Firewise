Why study flames in microgravity?

On Earth, buoyancy strongly influences how hot gases move around a flame. In microgravity, buoyancy-driven flow is greatly reduced. This changes the environment in which combustion occurs and makes controlled experiments valuable for understanding fire behaviour in spacecraft.

This is a simplified scientific introduction, not a summary of a particular experiment.

Mission relevance

Understanding combustion in spacecraft environments can contribute to research on material flammability, fire detection, prevention and crew safety. FIREWISE aims to make relevant research easier to explore and understand.
